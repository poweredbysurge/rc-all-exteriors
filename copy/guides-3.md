[H1] How Much Does a Roof Replacement Cost in Northern Virginia?

[BYLINE] By Ricardo Castro, owner of RC All Exteriors, Virginia Class A Contractor #2705196138 · Published November 2026

Intro

[P] The best published benchmark is [Remodeling magazine's Cost vs. Value report](https://www.jlconline.com/cost-vs-value/2025/). In 2025 it put the national average for an asphalt shingle roof replacement at $31,871 and a metal roof at $51,865. Your roof can land well under or over that. Size, steepness, the decking underneath and the material decide where.

[P] We don't publish our own prices, because two houses on the same street can need very different roofs. This guide shows what moves the number, so the estimate you get makes sense.

National numbers

[H2] The national numbers

[TABLE]
| Roof replacement (Remodeling, Cost vs. Value 2025, national) | Average job cost | Value added at resale | Cost recouped |
| Asphalt shingles | $31,871 | $21,501 | 68% |
| Metal | $51,865 | $25,972 | 50% |

[P] Those are averages across the country for a defined project, not our quote. They're useful as a sanity check: if one estimate is far off the others, ask what's in it and what isn't.

What moves the price

[H2] What moves the price

[LIST]

- **Size.** Roofers price by the "square," 100 square feet of roof. More squares, more shingles, more labor.
- **Steepness and shape.** A steep roof with hips, valleys and dormers takes more time and safety gear than a simple gable.
- **Layers coming off.** Tearing off two old layers costs more than one. Our [repair or replacement guide](/guides/roof-repair-or-replacement/) covers the two-layer rule.
- **Decking.** Rotted plywood has to be replaced. Our estimates include up to five sheets, and you see anything beyond that before we touch it.
- **Material.** CertainTeed Landmark is our standard architectural shingle. Landmark Pro, metal, Brava synthetic shake and cedar cost more.
- **Code items.** Virginia's building code requires an ice barrier at the eaves under shingles, and flashing around chimneys and skylights is replaced, not reused.
- **Permits.** Most shingle replacements in Northern Virginia need no permit. Replacing more than 256 square feet of decking does.
- **Extras done at the same time.** Gutters, skylights or siding cost less added to a roof job than as a second visit.

Repair or replace

[H2] Repair or replace first?

[P] The cheapest roof is the one you don't replace yet. If a repair will do, we say so and quote the repair. One customer: "Fixed my roof, didn't try to sell me a bunch of stuff I didn't need." Google review, December 2025.

Insurance

[H2] When insurance pays

[P] If wind, hail or a falling tree damaged the roof, your homeowners policy may pay for part or all of it, minus your deductible. If the roof just wore out, it usually won't. See [does insurance cover roof replacement](/guides/does-insurance-cover-roof-replacement/) and [how we help with claims](/insurance-claims/).

Comparing estimates

[H2] How to compare three estimates

[STEPS]

1. **Check the scope.** Same number of squares, same shingle line, same underlayment and ice barrier.
2. **Check the decking line.** How many sheets are included, and the price per sheet beyond that.
3. **Check the warranty.** The manufacturer's shingle warranty and the roofer's own workmanship warranty are separate. Ours is 10 years.
4. **Check the license.** Look the roofer up on [Virginia DPOR's license lookup](https://www.dpor.virginia.gov/LicenseLookup). Ours is Class A #2705196138.
5. **Watch for missing detail.** One homeowner got estimates of "$20k, $30K, or even $40K without any supporting, detailed estimates" before calling us. Rick Day, Google review, June 2026.

[P] On price, one customer told it straight: "Best of all their prices are 10-15% less than everyone else's." Bob Gull, Google review, May 2025.

FAQ

[H2] Questions homeowners ask us

[FAQ Q] How much does a new roof cost in Northern Virginia?

[FAQ A] Remodeling magazine's 2025 national average is $31,871 for asphalt shingles and $51,865 for metal. Northern Virginia jobs vary widely around that with roof size, steepness, decking and material. A free inspection gets you a written price for your roof.

[FAQ Q] Why don't you list your prices?

[FAQ A] Because a fair number depends on your roof. Two same-size houses can differ by thousands on decking alone. We'd rather give you one written, itemized price after we've been on the roof than a range that changes later.

[FAQ Q] Does a new roof add value to my house?

[FAQ A] Some. Remodeling's 2025 report found an asphalt roof replacement recouped 68% of its cost at resale, and a metal roof 50%. The bigger value is often avoiding a roof problem coming up in a buyer's inspection.

[FAQ Q] Can I finance a new roof?

[FAQ A] Yes. We offer financing. See [roof financing](/financing/) for how it works.

[FAQ Q] How long does a roof replacement take?

[FAQ A] Most of the time, one day on a typical house once work starts. Bigger or steeper roofs, rotted decking and weather can add time. Your written estimate includes the timeline.

[SHORT TITLE]

After the storm

[SUMMARY]

What to do in the first hour, day and week after hail or high wind hits your roof, and how to tell real damage from age.

[PUBLISHED]

2026-11-11

Hero

[H1] After the Storm: What to Do About Hail and Wind Roof Damage

[BYLINE] By Ricardo Castro, owner of RC All Exteriors, Virginia Class A Contractor #2705196138 · Published November 2026

Intro

[P] Not every storm damages a roof. The [National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/media/twc/WindHailReference.pdf) counts hail as severe at 1 inch, about the size of a quarter, and says winds of 58 to 73 mph remove shingles from houses. Below that, damage is less likely but still possible on an older roof.

[P] Here's what to do, in order, and how to tell storm damage from a roof that's simply getting old.

How strong

[H2] How strong is strong enough?

[TABLE]
| What hit your house | What the National Weather Service says it can do |
| Hail under 1 inch | Below its "severe" line. Older or brittle shingles can still bruise |
| Hail 1 inch (quarter) and up | Severe hail. The NWS asks people to report it right away |
| Wind 50 to 57 mph | "A few loose shingles removed from houses" |
| Wind 58 to 73 mph | "Shingles, awnings removed from houses" |
| Wind 74 mph and up | Roof damage to homes |

[IMAGE] Illustration: hail damage on asphalt shingles

First hour

[H2] The first hour

[STEPS]

1. **Stay off the roof.** Wet shingles are slick, and storm damage can hide soft decking.
2. **Photograph from the ground.** Shingles in the yard, dents in gutters and downspouts, ceiling stains. Your phone dates every picture.
3. **Stop water coming in.** Buckets inside, and call for a tarp. We answer emergency calls after hours and on weekends.

First two days

[H2] The first two days

[STEPS]

1. **Find the storm on record.** The [Storm Prediction Center's daily storm reports](https://www.spc.noaa.gov/climo/reports/) and [NOAA's Storm Events Database](https://www.ncdc.noaa.gov/stormevents/) list hail sizes and wind gusts by place and date. A dated report next to your photos ties the damage to that storm.
2. **Get a roof inspection.** Most of ours are scheduled within 24 to 48 hours. We go up, photograph every damaged area and tell you straight if it's storm damage or age.
3. **Decide on a claim.** If the damage is real and bigger than your deductible, open the claim. If it's small, a repair can cost you less than the deductible.

First week

[H2] The first week

[STEPS]

1. **Open the claim and get your adjuster's name.**
2. **Have your roofer at the adjuster meeting.** Two people looking at the same roof miss less.
3. **Compare estimates line by line.** See [how we help with insurance claims](/insurance-claims/).

Hail or wind

[H2] Hail or wind? How to tell from the ground

[LIST]

- **Hail:** dents on gutters, downspouts, vents and car hoods. On shingles, round dark spots where the granules were knocked off.
- **Wind:** missing shingles, shingles lifted or creased at the edges, ridge caps gone. Damage often lines up on the side the storm came from.
- **Age, not storm:** granules worn evenly across the whole roof, curling at every edge, and cracks that look the same on every side.

Storm chasers

[H2] Watch for storm chasers

[P] After a big storm, crews from out of the area knock on doors. Before anyone touches your roof, look up their license on [Virginia DPOR's license lookup](https://www.dpor.virginia.gov/LicenseLookup). Starting January 1, 2027, Virginia law bars contractors from paying your deductible or offering gifts for an inspection ([Code of Virginia § 59.1-618](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title59.1/chapter61/section59.1-618/)). Our [insurance guide](/guides/does-insurance-cover-roof-replacement/) covers the new rules.

[P] We've seen it play out. "When a windstorm damaged roofs throughout the region, we priced out repair options with various vendors and settled on Ricardo," one customer wrote. "Others added surcharges for a 'high' roof, as if all of them aren't high up." Brandon Renz, Google review, May 2025.

FAQ

[H2] Questions homeowners ask us

[FAQ Q] What size hail damages a roof?

[FAQ A] The National Weather Service calls hail severe at 1 inch, quarter size. Smaller hail can still bruise an older roof. Check dents on gutters and vents: if the soft metal is dented, the shingles took the same hits.

[FAQ Q] How do I know if wind damaged my roof?

[FAQ A] Look for missing shingles, shingles lifted or creased along the edges, missing ridge caps, and pieces in the yard. The National Weather Service says gusts of 58 to 73 mph remove shingles from houses. A creased shingle has lost its seal even if it lies flat now.

[FAQ Q] How do I prove the damage came from a specific storm?

[FAQ A] Pair your dated photos with the storm's record. The Storm Prediction Center's daily storm reports and NOAA's Storm Events Database list hail sizes and wind gusts by location and date. A roofer's dated inspection photos add the rest.

[FAQ Q] Should I let a door-to-door roofer inspect my roof?

[FAQ A] Check their Virginia license on DPOR first, and ask for a local address and references. Don't sign anything on the spot. From January 1, 2027, a contractor offering to cover your deductible is breaking Virginia law.

[FAQ Q] Can I wait until spring to deal with storm damage?

[FAQ A] Not safely. Water gets under lifted shingles every rain, and damage that grows while you wait can fall outside your policy. Get the inspection now, and at least tarp the opening.

[CTA] Not sure the storm did anything? Book a free storm inspection and get photos and a straight answer. Call or text (703) 677-1976.

Hero

[H1] Gutter Installation Cost: What New Gutters Cost Per Foot

[BYLINE] By Ricardo Castro, owner of RC All Exteriors, Virginia Class A Contractor #2705196138 · Published November 2026

Intro

[P] [Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/seamless-gutters-cost.htm) puts the average seamless gutter installation at $1,150, with most jobs between $650 and $1,700. Aluminum, the most common choice, runs $4 to $9 per linear foot. The length of your roofline, the number of downspouts and the material decide where your house lands.

[P] Those are Angi's national figures, not our quote. Here's what moves the number on a Northern Virginia house.

Cost per foot

[H2] Cost per foot by material

[TABLE]
| Material | Angi's range per linear foot |
| Vinyl | $3 to $5 |
| Aluminum | $4 to $9 |
| Galvanized steel | $8 to $10 |
| Copper | $15 to $25 |
| Zinc | $35 to $45 |

[P] We install seamless aluminum and copper. Aluminum suits most houses here. Copper costs about three times as much and is chosen mainly for its look.

What changes the total

[H2] What changes the total

[LIST]

- **Roofline length.** Gutters are priced by the foot, so a long ranch costs more than a compact colonial.
- **Height.** Two- and three-story runs need more ladder work and staging.
- **Downspouts.** Each one adds material and a drain point. Houses under trees often need more.
- **Gutter size.** 6 inch gutters carry more water than 5 inch, which matters on big or steep roofs.
- **Fascia.** If the boards behind the old gutters are rotted, they're replaced before new gutters go up.
- **Built-in or box gutters.** Older houses sometimes have gutters built into the roof edge. Repairing those is roofing work, not a simple swap.
- **Guards.** Leaf Relief guards add to the cost and cut cleaning. See our [gutter guards guide](/guides/gutter-guards-cost/).

Repair or replace

[H2] Repair or replace?

[P] Aluminum gutters last roughly 20 to 40 years ([InterNACHI](https://www.nachi.org/life-expectancy.htm)). Replace them when they pull away from the fascia, leak at several seams or overflow in normal rain because they're too small. A single loose hanger or one leaking seam is a repair.

A gutter job with us

[H2] What a gutter job looks like with us

[P] We measure the roofline, check the fascia and the roof edge, and give you a written price. Seamless gutters are formed in one continuous piece per run, so there are no seams along the length to leak.

[P] Most gutter jobs are quick. Kevin Jones replaced 22-year-old gutters with a 6 inch seamless system and leaf guard in March 2026, and the crew finished in under four hours, cleanup included. In June 2026, another customer wrote: "They helped fix a problem area and so far all is good." david flumer, Google review, June 2026.

[P] Older house with built-in gutters? We do those too: "My job was a built-in gutter repair... Fair pricing and the team worked efficiently." Kiesha M, Google review, October 2026.

FAQ

[H2] Questions homeowners ask us

[FAQ Q] How much do gutters cost per foot?

[FAQ A] Angi's figures: aluminum $4 to $9 per linear foot, galvanized steel $8 to $10, copper $15 to $25, vinyl $3 to $5. Height, downspouts and fascia repair change the total. We give a written price after measuring your house.

[FAQ Q] How much does it cost to replace gutters on a whole house?

[FAQ A] Angi's national average for seamless gutters is $1,150, with most jobs from $650 to $1,700. Bigger, taller houses and copper run higher. Rotted fascia behind the old gutters is the most common surprise, so we check it before we price.

[FAQ Q] Should I get 5 inch or 6 inch gutters?

[FAQ A] 6 inch gutters carry more water, so they suit big or steep roofs and houses where 5 inch gutters overflow in heavy rain. That was Kevin Jones's fix in 2026: a 6 inch system that "works great now in down pours."

[FAQ Q] Should I replace gutters with my roof?

[FAQ A] Often, yes. If the gutters are older or damaged, doing both together saves a second visit and lets us fit the new roof edge, drip edge and gutters to each other.

[FAQ Q] Are seamless gutters worth it?

[FAQ A] For most houses, yes. Sectional gutters have a joint every few feet, and each joint is a place to leak. Seamless gutters are formed in one piece per run, so the only joints are at corners and downspouts.
