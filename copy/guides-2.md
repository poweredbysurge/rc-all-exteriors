Gutter guards

[SUMMARY]

What gutter guards cost, the five main types, and whether they are worth it on a Northern Virginia house.

[PUBLISHED]

2026-11-02

Hero

[H1] Gutter Guards: What They Cost and Whether They're Worth It

[BYLINE] By Ricardo Castro, owner of RC All Exteriors, Virginia Class A Contractor #2705196138 · Published November 2026

Intro

[P] Gutter guards are worth it if your house sits under trees and your gutters overflow or fill up every fall. They are not worth it on gutters that are already sagging, leaking or near the end of their life. Fix or replace those first, then guard them.

[P] This guide covers what guards cost, how the main types compare, and what we install on Northern Virginia houses.

Cost

[H2] What gutter guards cost

[P] Price depends on the type, the length of your roofline, the height of the house and the shape of your gutters. [Angi's national figures](https://www.angi.com/articles/types-of-gutter-guards.htm) per linear foot, installed, give a fair sense of the spread:

[TABLE]
| Type | Angi's range per linear foot |
| Screen | $1 to $2.50 |
| Foam insert | $1.50 to $3 |
| Mesh | $2 to $7 |
| Brush insert | $3 to $6 |
| Micro-mesh | $2 to $8.50 |
| Reverse curve (surface tension) | $3.50 to $7 |

[P] Those are averages, not our quote. We give a written price for your house at the free inspection, after we measure the roofline and check the gutters the guards would sit on.

Types

[H2] The five main types, and what they handle

[LIST]

- Screen. A wire or plastic screen over the gutter. Cheap, but large holes let small debris through, and the screen comes off for cleaning.
- Foam and brush inserts. They sit inside the gutter. Quick to fit, but foam breaks down after a few seasons, and Angi notes that pine needles slip through brush inserts and tangle.
- Mesh and micro-mesh. A fine metal mesh over the top. Micro-mesh keeps out the smallest debris, but still needs a rinse about once a year.
- Reverse curve. Water follows the curved lip into the gutter while leaves slide off the edge. Good water flow, but it changes how the roof edge looks and is harder to install.
- Perforated metal covers. A solid cover with small holes. This is the family Leaf Relief belongs to.

Leaf Relief

[H2] What we install: Leaf Relief

[P] We install Leaf Relief, made by Ply Gem. It mounts straight onto the gutter with self-sealing screws, so we do not have to lift your shingles to fit it. It fits 5 and 6 inch gutters and adjusts to 4 inch, sits on continuous hangers that support the full length of the gutter, and carries the manufacturer's 25-year No-Clog, No-Overflow warranty.

[P] Leaf Relief covers smaller particles with a screen mesh as well as the perforated top, which matters on the oak and pine lots common in Woodbridge, Lake Ridge and Fairfax Station.

Guards or new gutters

[H2] Guards or new gutters?

[P] Check the gutters before you buy guards. Aluminum gutters have an estimated life of 20 to 40 years ([InterNACHI](https://www.nachi.org/life-expectancy.htm)). If yours are pulling away from the fascia, leaking at the seams or undersized for the roof, a guard will only hide the problem.

[P] One customer in March 2026 had replaced his steep roof with us two years earlier. When his 22-year-old gutters finally gave out, we put on a 6 inch seamless system with leaf guards. In his words: "Works great now in down pours and really improves the look of the roof as well." Kevin Jones, Google review, March 2026. The crew finished in under four hours, cleanup included.

FAQ

[H2] Questions homeowners ask us

[FAQ Q] Are gutter guards worth it?

[FAQ A] If trees overhang your roof and your gutters fill or overflow every fall, usually yes. They cut down on ladder trips and on water spilling behind the gutter onto the fascia. On a house with no trees nearby, a yearly cleaning is often cheaper. On worn-out gutters, replace the gutters first.

[FAQ Q] Do gutter guards work with pine needles?

[FAQ A] Some do better than others. Pine needles slip through brush inserts and large-hole screens. Fine mesh and perforated covers like Leaf Relief, which adds a screen mesh, keep most needles out. Expect to brush a few off the top after heavy needle drop.

[FAQ Q] Will gutter guards affect my roof warranty?

[FAQ A] Guards that slide under the first row of shingles mean lifting them. Leaf Relief mounts to the gutter itself with self-sealing screws, so the shingles are not touched. If your roof is under a manufacturer warranty, ask us at the inspection and we will check how your guards would attach.

[FAQ Q] Do gutter guards stop ice dams?

[FAQ A] No. Ice dams form when heat from the house melts snow on the roof and it refreezes at the colder eaves. The fix is attic insulation and ventilation, plus ice and water shield at the eaves on a new roof. Guards keep leaves out. They do not change how ice forms.

[FAQ Q] Do I still need to clean gutters with guards on?

[FAQ A] Less often, but yes. Even micro-mesh needs a rinse about once a year, and anything that sits on top of a guard after a storm can be brushed off. What you skip is scooping packed leaves out of the trough.

[SHORT TITLE]

Repair or replace?

[SUMMARY]

How to tell whether your roof needs a repair or a full replacement, from the age of the shingles to Virginia's two-layer rule.

[PUBLISHED]

2026-11-02

Hero

[H1] Roof Repair or Replacement? How to Decide

[BYLINE] By Ricardo Castro, owner of RC All Exteriors, Virginia Class A Contractor #2705196138 · Published November 2026

Intro

[P] Repair it if the damage is in one spot and the rest of the roof has years left. Replace it if the shingles are near the end of their life, if leaks keep coming back in different places, or if the deck underneath is soft. Most roofs we inspect clearly fall on one side or the other.

[P] Here is how we decide, so you can check our thinking against your own roof.

Quick test

[H2] The quick test

[TABLE]
| Repair usually makes sense | Replacement usually makes sense |
| One leak, one area, one cause | Leaks in more than one place, or back after a repair |
| A few shingles blown off in a storm | Shingles curling, cracking or shedding granules across the roof |
| Flashing or a pipe boot has failed | Architectural shingles near 30 years, 3-tab near 20 |
| Deck is solid when we walk it | Deck is soft or sagging in more than one spot |
| Matching shingles are still made | Your shingle is discontinued and the patch would not match |

Age

[H2] How old is your roof?

[P] Age is the first thing we look at. [InterNACHI's life expectancy chart](https://www.nachi.org/life-expectancy.htm) puts architectural asphalt shingles at about 30 years and 3-tab shingles at about 20. Virginia's hot summers, freeze and thaw winters and tree cover can shorten that.

[P] If your roof is past those marks, a repair buys you time, not a roof. That can still be the right call if you are selling soon or saving up. We will tell you roughly how much time it buys.

What we check

[H2] What we check on the roof

[LIST]

- Shingles. Curling, cracking, bald spots where the granules are gone, and how many are missing.
- Flashing. Chimneys, walls, skylights and valleys are where most leaks start, and many can be fixed without touching the rest of the roof.
- Decking. We walk the roof and check the attic for soft spots, stains and daylight.
- Layers. How many roofs are already on the house. See the next section.
- Ventilation. A hot, damp attic ages shingles from underneath.

Two-layer rule

[H2] The two-layer rule

[P] Virginia's residential building code is based on the International Residential Code. Under its reroofing rules, you cannot add a new layer of shingles over a roof that already has two or more layers, or over one that is water-soaked or too deteriorated to hold new roofing. In those cases the old layers have to come off.

[P] We tear off to the deck on every replacement anyway. It is the only way to see and fix rotted boards, and it is how our 10-year workmanship warranty is backed.

Matching

[H2] When a repair won't match

[P] A patch only works if the new shingles match the old ones. When a shingle line is discontinued, a repair can stand out, and on a storm claim that can change what your insurer pays for.

[P] One customer had a wind damage claim where the insurer first approved only sections of shingles. Those shingles were no longer made and the new ones were a different size. After we documented it, the claim covered a full roof, the mansards, the carport and 12 sheets of plywood. "Ricardo's determined efforts in dealing with the insurer resulted in a new full roof." John W Allen, Google review, October 2025.

Permits and time

[H2] Permits, warranty and what each option costs you in time

[P] In [Fairfax County](https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/landdevelopment/when-permit-required) and [Prince William County](https://www.pwcva.gov/assets/2021-04/roofreplacements.pdf), replacing roof shingles does not need a building permit. Replacing a large section of decking does: 256 square feet or more in both counties. Fairfax County also lists roof repairs as permit work. We tell you up front if your job needs one, and we pull it.

[P] A repair is usually a half-day or one-day job. A full replacement on a typical house is usually one to two days, longer on a large or steep roof. Our repairs carry a 60-day warranty on the area we fixed. A new roof carries our 10-year workmanship warranty and the shingle manufacturer's own warranty.

Recent repair

[H2] From a recent repair

[P] Not every inspection ends in a new roof. "Ricardo is just great! Fixed my roof, didn't try to sell me a bunch of stuff I didn't need." M B, Google review, December 2025.

FAQ

[H2] Questions homeowners ask us

[FAQ Q] Is it cheaper to repair or replace a roof?

[FAQ A] A repair costs less today. A replacement costs less over time if the roof is near the end of its life, because repeated repairs add up and each one only covers the spot that was fixed. We price both when both are real options, so you can compare.

[FAQ Q] Can you put new shingles over my old ones?

[FAQ A] Only if there is just one layer now and it is dry and sound. Virginia's building code does not allow a new layer over two or more existing layers or over a deteriorated roof. We tear off to the deck on every replacement so we can check and fix the boards underneath.

[FAQ Q] How long does a roof replacement take?

[FAQ A] Most houses take one to two days. Large, steep or complex roofs, bad weather and extra decking repairs can add time. You get a start date with your estimate, and the crew cleans up the same day the work finishes.

[FAQ Q] Will insurance pay to replace my roof instead of repairing it?

[FAQ A] Insurance pays to put the roof back to its condition before the storm. That can mean a repair or a full roof, depending on how much is damaged and whether the shingles can be matched. Our guide to [whether insurance covers roof replacement in Virginia](/guides/does-insurance-cover-roof-replacement/) explains how it works.

[FAQ Q] Is there a warranty on a roof repair?

[FAQ A] Yes, 60 days on the area we repaired. A repair fixes one spot on a roof that keeps aging at its own pace, so we cannot cover the rest of the roof the way we do with a full replacement. If the roof is close to the end of its life, we say so.
