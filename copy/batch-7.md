Permits and HOA rules

[H2] Permits and HOA rules for a new roof in Fairfax Station

[P] Fairfax County does not require a building permit to replace roof shingles. It does once the job turns into a roof repair, and once the plywood decking being replaced reaches 256 square feet, about nine sheets. If the deck under your shingles is worse than it looks, we tell you before the work starts and we pull the permit ourselves.

[P] Your HOA may have its own rules. Pohick Station's, for example, lets owners replace a roof with cedar shake or architectural shingles without filing an application, as long as the shingles are a dark earth tone like grey, brown or black and the flashing matches. Any other material needs the committee's approval first. Send us your HOA's guidelines and we will quote a roof that passes them.

Shake roofs

[H2] Cedar shake, or something that looks like it

[P] Some Fairfax Station homes still wear cedar shake. Wood roofs have an estimated life of about 25 years (InterNACHI), and when one is done, you have three choices: new cedar, architectural shingles, or a synthetic shake. We are a certified Brava installer. Brava synthetic shake looks like cedar, carries a 50-year limited warranty, and does not need the upkeep wood does.

[P] Check your HOA first. Under rules like Pohick Station's, a synthetic shake counts as "any other roofing material" and needs approval, while cedar and architectural shingles do not. We give every homeowner Brava samples and product literature, so you have what your committee asks for.

FAQ

[H2] Fairfax Station roofing questions

[FAQ Q] Do I need a permit to replace my roof in Fairfax Station?

[FAQ A] Usually not. Fairfax County does not require a building permit to replace roof shingles. A permit is needed for roof repairs and once the decking being replaced reaches 256 square feet. If your job crosses that line, we pull the permit. You can confirm with Fairfax County Land Development Services at 703-222-0801.

[FAQ Q] Does my HOA have to approve a new roof?

[FAQ A] Read your HOA's guidelines first. Pohick Station's, for example, needs no application for cedar shake or architectural shingles in dark earth tones, but does for any other material. Send us your guidelines and we will quote a roof and color that fit them.

[FAQ Q] My cedar shake roof is worn out. What should I replace it with?

[FAQ A] New cedar, architectural shingles or a synthetic shake. Cedar keeps the original look but needs upkeep. Architectural shingles cost least. Brava synthetic shake looks like cedar and carries a 50-year limited warranty. We price the options side by side at your inspection.

[FAQ Q] How fast can you get to Fairfax Station for an inspection?

[FAQ A] Call or text (703) 677-1976. Most inspections are scheduled within 24 to 48 hours. We come straight up Route 123 from our base in Woodbridge.

[FAQ Q] Do you help with insurance claims after a storm?

[FAQ A] Yes. We inspect the roof, photograph the damage, write an itemized estimate and meet your adjuster if you want us there. Our guide to [whether insurance covers roof replacement in Virginia](/guides/does-insurance-cover-roof-replacement/) explains how a claim goes.
