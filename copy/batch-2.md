Intro

[P] A drip in the ceiling after a storm. A bucket on the floor. A stain that keeps getting bigger. Once water is inside, every hour adds to the repair bill.

[P] Call or text (703) 677-1976 and tell us what you're seeing. We'll get someone out, track down where the water is getting in, and protect the house until the permanent repair. Emergency calls are answered after hours and on weekends, and we tell you a real arrival time when you call.

What to do now

[H2] While you wait for us

[STEPS]

1.  Catch the water. Buckets and towels under the drip. Move furniture and electronics out of the way.
2.  Relieve the bulge. If the ceiling is sagging with water, poke a small hole at the lowest point over a bucket. That keeps the drywall from coming down.
3.  Stay off the roof. A wet roof is slippery, and storms can leave loose pieces up there.
4.  Take photos. Inside and out, with the date. Your insurance company will want them.

What we do

[H2] How we handle a roof leak

[LIST]

- Leak detection. Water travels, so the leak is often not right above the stain. We trace it back to the real source.
- Temporary protection. If a full repair can't happen right away, we tarp the damaged area to keep water out.
- Permanent repair. Shingles, flashing, vents, or chimney, fixed with the most durable matching materials we can get.
- Insurance help. If a storm caused it, we help with the estimate and the insurance claim.

[LINK] Storm damage and insurance → /storm-damage-roof-repair/

Common causes

[H2] Where most roof leaks start

[LIST]

- Missing or blown-off shingles after wind
- Flashing that has pulled away around chimneys and walls
- Cracked rubber boots around plumbing vents
- Worn shingles on an older roof
- Clogged gutters backing water under the roof edge

FAQ

[H2] Emergency repair questions

[FAQ Q] Can you come out today?

[FAQ A] Call or text (703) 677-1976 and we'll tell you how fast we can get there. Our office hours are Monday to Friday, 7am to 5pm, and Saturday, 8am to 4pm, and emergency calls are answered after hours and on Sundays. We give you a real arrival time instead of a promise we can't keep.

[FAQ Q] How much does an emergency roof repair cost?

[FAQ A] It depends on what caused the leak and how much damage the water did. A temporary tarp costs less than a permanent repair. We'll give you a written price before we start, and the repaired area carries a 60-day warranty.

[FAQ Q] Will insurance pay for a leaking roof?

[FAQ A] If a storm caused the damage, often yes. If the leak comes from age and wear, usually not. We can tell you which it looks like after we inspect. Our guide to [whether insurance covers roof replacement in Virginia](/guides/does-insurance-cover-roof-replacement/) walks through the rules.

[FAQ Q] Is a tarp a permanent fix?

[FAQ A] No. A tarp keeps water out for a short time until the real repair. We'll schedule the permanent fix as soon as we can.

Two warranties

[H2] Your roof is covered two ways

[H3] Our 10-year workmanship warranty

[P] This is RC All Exteriors' own warranty, separate from the CertainTeed manufacturer warranty. It covers the installation itself: how the shingles, flashing, and vents were put on. If a problem traces back to our work within 10 years, we fix it. The warranty transfers one time if you sell the house.

[H3] The CertainTeed manufacturer warranty

[P] CertainTeed covers the shingles and roofing materials against manufacturing defects. If you choose the extended warranty, we install the roof as a full CertainTeed Integrity Roof System, and it qualifies for SureStart PLUS 3-Star coverage: materials, labor, tear-off, and disposal for 50 years, an unlimited wind warranty, and a one-time transfer within 15 years. [LINK] CertainTeed roofing → /certainteed-roofing/

What it means for you

[H2] What this means in plain terms

[LIST]

- A leak caused by how we installed the roof: covered by us
- A defective shingle: covered by CertainTeed
- Damage from a storm, a fallen tree, or foot traffic: not a warranty issue. It's usually an insurance claim, and we can help with that
- Normal wear after decades of weather: not covered by either

[P] A repair carries its own 60-day warranty on the area we repaired.

[H2] How to make a warranty claim

2.  We inspect. We come out and find the cause.
3.  We fix it. If it's our workmanship, the repair is on us.

[P] Keep your warranty paperwork with your house records. On a CertainTeed install you also receive the registered CertainTeed warranty paperwork, which we file for you.

FAQ

[H2] Warranty questions

[FAQ Q] Does the warranty transfer if I sell my house?

[FAQ A] Yes, once. Our 10-year workmanship warranty transfers one time to the next owner. If your roof is registered for CertainTeed SureStart PLUS 3-Star coverage, that also transfers once within the first 15 years.

[FAQ Q] Do repairs come with a warranty too?

[FAQ A] Yes, on the area we repaired, for 60 days. A repair fixes one spot on a roof that is otherwise aging at its own pace, so we can't cover the rest of the roof the way we do with a full replacement. If the roof is near the end of its life, we'll say so.

[FAQ Q] Why does a workmanship warranty matter?

[FAQ A] Many roof problems in the first years come from how the roof was installed, not from the shingles. A manufacturer warranty alone doesn't cover installation mistakes. That's why ours matters.
