What to do now

[H2] While you wait for us

[STEPS]

1.  Catch the water. Buckets and towels under the drip. Move furniture and electronics out of the way.
2.  Relieve the bulge. If the ceiling is sagging with water, poke a small hole at the lowest point over a bucket. That keeps the drywall from coming down.
3.  Stay off the roof. A wet roof is slippery, and storms can leave loose pieces up there.
4.  Take photos. Inside and out, with the date. Your insurance company will want them.

What we do

[H2] How we handle a roof leak

[LIST]

- Leak detection. Water travels, so the leak is often not right above the stain. We trace it back to the real source.
- Temporary protection. If a full repair can't happen right away, we tarp the damaged area to keep water out. [CONFIRM: emergency tarping offered]
- Permanent repair. Shingles, flashing, vents, or chimney, fixed with the most durable matching materials we can get.
- Insurance help. If a storm caused it, we help with the estimate and the insurance claim.

Common causes

[H2] Where most roof leaks start

[LIST]

- Missing or blown-off shingles after wind
- Flashing that has pulled away around chimneys and walls
- Cracked rubber boots around plumbing vents
- Worn shingles on an older roof
- Clogged gutters backing water under the roof edge
