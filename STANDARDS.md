# STANDARDS.md: what the next client build should inherit

Every entry is something this build needed that is not specific to RC All Exteriors. Status is `candidate` until it graduates into the master website prompt, then `promoted`.

| # | Standard | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Parse the copy doc, do not retype it.** `src/lib/copy.ts` reads the `/copy` pipeline's tagged batches (`[H1]`, `[P]`, `[LIST]`, `[FAQ Q]`, `[CONFIRM: ...]`) into typed blocks. Pages render blocks. | 24 pages of copy landed verbatim, FAQ schema cannot drift from the accordion, and a copy revision is a file swap. The parser throws on any tag or line it cannot place. | candidate |
| 2 | **The copy doc's tag format is now an interface.** Batch 1 uses bold tags and a spec table; batches 2 to 5 use plain tags and key/value lines. The parser handles both. Pick one in the `/copy` skill. | Two formats doubled the parser. | candidate |
| 3 | **`[CONFIRM]` is a first-class element.** Rendered as a visible dashed placeholder with `data-confirm`, counted per page by the gate, a hard failure with `--production`. Photo gaps, review slots and unapproved legal text use the same attribute. | 222 open slots on this build, findable with one search, and none can ship. | candidate |
| 4 | **Generate the Tailwind theme from the design tokens file.** `scripts/sync-tokens.mjs` turns `design/tokens.css` into the `@theme` block; the gate fails if it is stale. Have Mode A name tokens in Tailwind 4 namespaces (`--color-*`, `--radius-*`, `--shadow-*`, `--font-*`). | The design folder is read-only, so the theme has to be derived, not copied. | candidate |
| 5 | **Measure fallback font metrics in a browser, over the site's own copy.** `scripts/measure-fallbacks.mjs`. | Archivo Expanded is 25% wider than Arial Bold. A generic average-width ratio would have been several points off; measured, CLS is 0. | candidate |
| 6 | **Check the font subset for the glyphs the design uses.** The Latin subsets here have no U+2011 (non-breaking hyphen) and no arrow. Use `white-space: nowrap` and icon arrows instead. | A missing glyph renders in the fallback face mid-word. | candidate |
| 7 | **Headings in sentence case are derived, with a proper-noun list.** `sentenceCase()` in `src/lib/text.ts`. | The copy writes H1s in title case for the title tag; the design sets them in sentence case. | candidate |
| 8 | **Art-direct the mobile hero.** A portrait crop sized to the viewport behind the headline, fading into the dark surface; the wide photo only from the two-column breakpoint. Two preloads with `media`. | A landscape photo stretched behind a 2,000px stacked hero is a blurry or heavy LCP. Mobile LCP here is 2.1 to 2.4s on Lighthouse throttling. | candidate |
| 9 | **One LCP map, two readers.** `LCP_MAP` feeds both the preload in the layout and the hero component; a route with no entry fails the build; `text` marks pages whose LCP is the H1. | The preload cannot point at a different file than the hero. | candidate |
| 10 | **Prune originals from the output.** Astro copies every imported source image next to its derivatives. `scripts/prune-originals.mjs` runs after `astro build`. | Otherwise 4MB of JPG ships and the "no JPG" gate fails. | candidate |
| 11 | **No UI framework for a brochure site.** Lead form, nav, accordion, dropdown and sticky bar in about 6KB of plain TypeScript. | The 60KB JS budget with 90% to spare. | candidate |
| 12 | **Reserve the sticky header's height on the outer element and shrink an inner bar.** | A shrinking sticky header otherwise shifts the page on scroll, which counts toward CLS. | candidate |
| 13 | **Hide collapsed content with CSS gated on a `js` class set in the head**, for FAQ panels and the mobile footer accordion. | All answers stay in the HTML for search and for no-JS; nothing collapses after paint. | candidate |
| 14 | **A lead is never dropped behind a success message.** If no sink accepts it the route returns 503, the form shows retry copy with the phone number and queues the payload for replay. | "Routes degrade gracefully" must not mean "leads vanish quietly" on a preview with no env vars. | candidate |
| 15 | **Server stamps `source_detail`, IP and user agent; the response returns `source_detail`** so GA4 `generate_lead` can carry it without the browser deriving it. | One implementation of the attribution priority order. | candidate |
| 16 | **Render the consent label from the versioned consent string.** | Text and `consent_text_version` cannot drift. | candidate |
| 17 | **Review counts written into copy follow the live count.** `liveCount()` rewrites "49 five-star reviews" style phrases at build from the reviews proxy, in visible text and schema. | "Update the number at launch" becomes automatic. | candidate |
| 18 | **Two gates.** `check-integrity` (static: structure, schema, links, inlinks, CONFIRM, assets, source rules) and `check-layout` (browser: overflow at 15 widths, computed type floor, header switch, tap targets, sticky CTA). | The layout half needs a browser; keeping it separate keeps the static half fast enough to run on every build. | candidate |
| 19 | **Copy-owned length misses are reported, not auto-fixed.** Titles and descriptions outside 50 to 60 and 140 to 160 that come verbatim from approved copy are listed for the copy owner in preview and fail only with `--production`. | The build must not rewrite approved copy to satisfy a counter. | candidate |
| 20 | **Small text is opt-in.** Any `<p>` at 16px carries `data-sm`; the gate fails an unmarked one. | The 18px body floor is enforceable instead of aspirational. | candidate |
| 21 | **Old-site sitemap first.** Pull `wp-sitemap.xml` (or equivalent) on day one and author `deploy/redirects.json` from it, parked behind `doNotDeployYet`. | The prompt listed 8 old URLs from memory; the sitemap had 11, and the slugs differed. | candidate |
| 22 | **Strip metadata from logo SVG exports.** The supplied SVGs carried 11KB of C2PA manifest each. | 19KB to 7.5KB per logo. | candidate |
| 23 | **Legal page templates do not exist in the workspace yet.** The master prompt points at `templates/legal`. Build that folder from this site's three pages once RC approves them. | Every build needs privacy with an SMS section, terms and SMS terms. | candidate |
| 24 | **Astro `trailingSlash: 'always'` also applies to API routes.** Post to `/api/lead/`. | A POST to the slashless path is redirected. | candidate |
| 25 | **Post-Gate-A token changes go in `brand/tokens.overrides.css`**, same format as `design/tokens.css`, read second by `sync-tokens` so its values win. Each entry names its ADR. | `design/` stays read-only and the theme still has one generator. | promoted (master prompt 1.3, section 22) |
| 26 | **The hero fits the first viewport.** `clamp(min, 100svh - header, max)` on the section, not `min-height` inside padded sections; `check-layout` asserts hero and card bottoms at 1440 by 900 and 1920 by 1080. | A 640px grid inside 128px padding plus a 900px card is a 1,150px hero. | promoted (master prompt 1.3, section 10 and 21) |
| 27 | **The trust row is its own strip under the hero**, chip plus three items. | The hero holds four things and fits; the strip carries trust at full width. | promoted (master prompt 1.3, section 10) |
| 28 | **Lead card as panes, contract unchanged.** `initPanes()` in `src/scripts/lead.ts` pages any form's `[data-pane]` groups; pane 1 radios advance on change; step 2 button flips Next to Finish on the last pane. HomeSource `initQuizPanes` plus Sunrise's one-tap first question. | Step 1 at 560px instead of 900px; nothing about the payload moved. | promoted (master prompt 1.3, section 10) |
| 29 | **Dark header by default, logo never under 44px**, inverse CTA on dark. Heights and logo sizes are tokens, not literals in the component. | A wide lockup at 40px is unreadable; the accent button disappears on the dark surface. | promoted (master prompt 1.3, section 7) |
| 30 | **`ServiceCard` takes a `photo` prop** and becomes image-led (16:10, no icon). The homepage uses it; hubs keep the icon card. | Services are the first thing after the hero and a photo says more than a line icon. | promoted (master prompt 1.3, section 18) |
| 31 | **`Eyebrow onDark`.** Accent blue on the dark surface does not pass; the eyebrow goes white there. | Needed the moment a section with an eyebrow went dark. | candidate |
| 32 | **`check-layout` takes `CHROME_ARGS`** (for example `--no-sandbox` in a container running as root) beside `CHROME_PATH`. | The gate has to run where the build runs. | candidate |
| 33 | **Surface rhythm and the opening photo are planned from the copy's section kinds** (`src/lib/rhythm.ts`: dark band in the first half, two lights before the CTA band, band moves below the FAQ when needed, companion photo by job type). | Eleven service pages from one template got the homepage's depth with no per-page decisions. | promoted (master prompt 1.3, section 5) |
| 34 | **One `.on-dark` inversion rule for the fg utilities**, unlayered, skipping self-surfaced elements (`.bg-surface`, `.card`, `.dropdown-panel`, `[data-lead-card]`). | Any section can go dark without a dark variant per component. | promoted (master prompt 1.3, section 5) |
| 35 | **`scripts/audit-rhythm.mjs`** prints every page's surface sequence and photo count. | The rhythm rules are checkable in one screen. | candidate |
| 36 | **Nav menus render each service's registry icon** (`services[].icon` in `src/data/nav.ts`), 20px, accent, in the dropdown and the mobile list. | One registry feeds cards, menus and footer; the menu scans as services, not links. | promoted (master prompt 1.3, section 7) |
| 37 | **A portrait photo used as a desktop hero needs a pre-cut 16:9 `heroSrc`.** Astro's image service takes keyword positions only (top, centre), so `position: 'center 28%'` is ignored and a portrait source is cropped through its middle. Cut the band once with sharp and register it; the portrait stays for the 3:4 mobile crop. | The first hero attempt showed siding and a truck instead of the roof. | candidate |
| 38 | **Originals capped at 2400px and the build run with `SHARP_CONCURRENCY=1`** once the registry passes about 20 photos. | 30 photos at 3200px killed an 8GB build container; 610 derivatives at one thread take 25 minutes and finish. | candidate |
| 39 | **Placeholder components degrade to nothing at launch.** `PhotoGap`, `ReviewCard` without a review, and a `Figure` with no town render nothing; the `data-confirm` gate still exists for preview and reports zero. | A launch cannot show a labeled slot, and the gate must not be bypassed by deleting it. | promoted (master prompt 1.3, section 16) |
| 40 | **`[CTA] Label → https://...` is a valid external CTA in the copy format**; the parser accepts absolute URLs and the button opens them in a new tab. | Financing applications and review links live off-site. | candidate |
| 41 | **Every photo with a person names a crop anchor** (`focus` in `photos.ts`) and `sourceSet` passes it to sharp. Centre is the default and it beheads a standing person in a landscape crop. | The step 1 photo on the homepage showed a torso. | promoted (master prompt 1.4, section 13) |
| 42 | **Nobody is named in captions or alt text.** "An RC All Exteriors professional", "the crew", "on site". Photo keys are `pro*` and `crew*`, not a person's name. | Three "Ricardo" photos were his brother-in-law. | promoted (master prompt 1.4, section 13) |
| 43 | **Hero CTAs stack full width below `lg`.** The old `btn:` (480px) breakpoint put two long labels side by side on big phones. | Four lines of wrapped button at 430px. | promoted (master prompt 1.4, section 10) |
| 44 | **Mobile hero scrim at 45 percent with a text shadow on the H1 and lede.** | At 60 percent the photo was a navy wash. | promoted (master prompt 1.4, section 10) |
| 45 | **`.table-wrap[data-stack]` turns a comparison table into cards below 640px**, cells headed by `data-label`. | The repair-or-replace table lost its third column at 390. | promoted (master prompt 1.4, section 5) |
| 46 | **Reviews wall without reviews is one card to the Google profile**, plus a "Leave a Google review" chip. | "In their own words" rendered as a heading over nothing until the Places key lands. | promoted (master prompt 1.4, section 16) |
| 47 | **Social profiles in copy lists are markdown links**, not `→ url`. | The contact page printed the Facebook URL as text. | promoted (master prompt 1.4, section 16) |
| 48 | **Review cards carry Google's cues**: `--color-star` yellow, reviewer photo, link to the review (`googleMapsUri`), eight-line clamp with "Read more" over 320 characters. `Review` type has `photo` and `url`. | The first live wall had blue stars, no faces, no link, and one essay stretching the row. | promoted (master prompt 1.4, section 12) |
| 49 | **`liveCount` replaces any literal count before "five-star", "Google" or "reviews"**, not only the fallback number. | Updating `fallbackCount` silently broke the replacement. | promoted (master prompt 1.4, section 12) |
| 50 | **The pullquote slot prefers a review under 50 words** (`pickReviews` `pullquote` option); a long one drops a type step and clamps. | A 170-word review at H3 filled the homepage. | promoted (master prompt 1.4, section 12) |
